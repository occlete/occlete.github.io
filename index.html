About Me

I am a master's student in Computer Science and Technology at Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics (2023–2026), advised by Prof. Pan Gao at the Immersive and Interactive Multimedia Lab (I2ML).

My current research interests are 3D vision tasks, especially 3D/4D generation, reconstruction, and understanding. I am also interested in learning representations.
